A simulated two-period panel of 2,199 workers in which the true union effect is 0.21 log points. Change how union membership is selected on the worker effect αi, how many workers switch status, and how noisy wages are, then compare four estimators.

−0.15

Correlation between αi and the propensity to be a union member. Negative values mean that lower-wage workers are more likely to be union members, as in the post. Default −0.15.

3.3%

Workers whose union status changes between the two periods; only they identify FE. Default 3.3%, the 73 switchers of the post.

0.30

Standard deviation of the period-specific wage shock εit. More noise widens every interval, the FE interval most of all. Default 0.30.
