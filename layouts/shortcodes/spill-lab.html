Plug-in values at the posterior means

The effect on California travels to Nevada with strength ρ and then ripples through the contiguity graph of the 38 donors. Move ρ, the size of the effect and the number of ripple rounds, or start the ripple in another state, and watch every donor respond.

0.316

Default 0.316, the posterior mean; the 95% credible interval runs from 0.231 to 0.403. The package bounds ρ below 0.95.

−16.87

Default −16.87, the purged ATT for California. Every spillover is proportional to it.

all (exact)

Round k reaches the states k steps from the origin. The far right position solves the system exactly.
