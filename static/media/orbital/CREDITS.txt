Earth at night imagery
======================
NASA Earth Observatory / Black Marble, 2016 global nighttime-light composite.
These are historical composite images, not current or live observations.

Source pages:
https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/earth-at-night/maps/
https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/earth-at-night/spheres-and-animations/

black-marble-2016.jpg (3600 x 1800):
https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/imagerecords/144000/144898/BlackMarble_2016_01deg.jpg

black-marble-2016-mobile.jpg (1800 x 900, NASA image service):
https://assets.science.nasa.gov/dynamicimage/assets/science/esd/eo/images/imagerecords/144000/144898/BlackMarble_2016_01deg.jpg?w=1800&h=900&fit=clip

Static Asia globe source, stored in assets/media/orbital/earth-at-night-asia.png
and converted to WebP by Hugo:
https://assets.science.nasa.gov/dynamicimage/assets/science/esd/eo/content-feature/nightlights/images/media/BlackMarble_2016_Asia_composite.png?w=992&h=800&fit=clip

The interactive globe projects the real map onto a sphere with a subtle
illustrative atmospheric rim. No generated or simulated light locations are
added. Decorative rings and grids are interface elements, not observations.
Brightness is a qualitative indicator of human activity, not a direct measure
of income. The image is not a calibrated radiance dataset or analytical tool.
Retrieved 2026-10-03.

Generated decorative imagery
=============================
The following homepage assets were generated with OpenAI's built-in image
generation tool on 2026-10-04 and are illustrative rather than observational
data or official ESA/NASA mission photography:

- assets/media/orbital/sentinel-2-foreground-v1.png
- assets/media/orbital/sentinel-2-distant-v1.png
- assets/media/orbital/earth-night-asia-footer-v1.png

The spacecraft are scientifically grounded Sentinel-2 visualizations without
logos or labels. The footer is a cinematic East Asia and Japan night horizon;
its light locations are illustrative and must not be used for analysis. Hugo
creates the smaller WebP files served by the homepage.
